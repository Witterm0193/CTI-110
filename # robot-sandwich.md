# Robot Sandwich Instructions

## Assumptions

- The robot begins standing in front of a clean kitchen counter.
- The robots hands are empty.
- A loaf of sliced bread is inside a plastic bread bag on the counter.
- Peanut butter is in a closed jar on the counter.
- Jelly is in a closed jar on the counter.
- A clean plate and clean butter knife are on the counter.
- All containers are within reach of the robot.

## Steps

1. Pick up the bread bag with your left hand.
2. Use your right hand to remove the closure from the bread bag.
3. Place the closure on the counter.
4. Pull the opening of the bread bag apart.
5. Remove two individual slices of bread from the bag.
6. Place both slices flat on the plate without stacking them.
7. Place the bread bag back on the counter.
8. Pick up the peanut butter jar.
9. Rotate the lid counterclockwise until it separates from the jar.
10. Place the lid on the counter.
11. Pick up the butter knife by its handle.
12. Insert the blade into the peanut butter.
13. Remove some peanut butter with the blade.
14. Spread the peanut butter across one slice of bread.
15. Place the knife on the plate.
16. Replace the peanut butter lid.
17. Pick up the jelly jar.
18. Rotate the lid counterclockwise until it separates from the jar.
19. Place the lid on the counter.
20. Pick up the knife by its handle.
21. Insert the blade into the jelly.
22. Remove some jelly with the blade.
23. Spread the jelly across the second slice of bread.
24. Place the knife on the plate.
25. Replace the jelly lid.
26. Pick up the slice of bread with jelly.
27. Turn it so the jelly side faces downward.
28. Place it on top of the peanut butter slice.
29. Release the bread.
30. Leave the completed sandwich on the plate.

## Done When

The job is finished when two slices of bread are stacked together on the plate with the peanut butter and jelly between them.
